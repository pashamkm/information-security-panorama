The InfoSec Enclave

Trying to understand information security all at once causes cognitive overload. This tool uses progressive disclosure to connect the dots gradually through a 100-house gated community.

How to Use This App
1. Anchor to the Mental Model Replace abstract terminology with an intuitive foundation: enterprise defense as a living 100-house estate.
2. Decode One Domain at a Time Explore buzzwords (IAM, SOC, AppSec) in the Enclave Decoder to see their neighborhood equivalents light up.
3. Inspect the Frameworks Examine formal industry standards (NIST CSF 2.0, ISO 27001, OWASP, Zero Trust) without drowning in jargon.
"Can we understand the world of Information Security at a glance? Probably not. Trying to absorb every discipline at once doesn't bring clarity—it creates cognitive overload."
IAM, SOC, GRC, Cloud, AppSec, DFIR, OT, DevSecOps… our industry has exploded into an intimidating alphabet soup. When presented all at once, beginners see disjointed silos, and even seasoned practitioners can lose sight of the foundational architecture.

Real understanding doesn't happen in a single glance. It happens gradually, by anchoring unfamiliar technical ideas to something we already understand intuitively.
Picture an enterprise as a gated community of 100 houses. Every organization has perimeter gates, shared utility lines, property records, and human residents. By stepping into this neighborhood layer by layer, each tool and framework falls cleanly into place as a practical, commonsense duty required to build, govern, and defend a shared habitat.
